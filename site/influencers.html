03 · Promotion strategies

Six formats, one campaign

Photo and text posts, reels, insertion videos, full videos and livestreams — picked to match the platform, the market and the creator.

The original guide worked from three key mediums for an aspiring influencer: photo posting, text sharing and reels content. Each has a short method behind it, and the guide sets out how to use them to maximise promotional impact.

Photo post

Capture the essence of the casino: vibrant photos of gaming floors, exclusive events and interiors.

Text post

Pair the visual with a compelling caption to lift engagement on the same creative.

Reels video

Short-form video that shows different sides of the platform. Music and creative transitions carry the watch.

Insertion video

An advertisement inserted into the creator's own content, popping up to showcase the promotion.

Full video

A dedicated video produced entirely about the promotion.

Livestreaming

The creator plays the game live, so viewers see the platform working before they try it.
